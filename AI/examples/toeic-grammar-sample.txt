TOEIC Grammar Sample: Present Perfect, For, and Since

The present perfect uses have or has plus the past participle. It commonly describes an
action that started in the past and continues now, or a past action with a present result.

Use "for" with a duration:
- The sales manager has worked here for three years.
- We have waited for twenty minutes.

Use "since" with a starting point:
- The sales manager has worked here since 2023.
- The office has been open since 8 a.m.

Use the simple past, not the present perfect, with an explicitly finished past time:
- Correct: She submitted the report yesterday.
- Incorrect: She has submitted the report yesterday.
